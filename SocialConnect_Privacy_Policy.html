1. Introduction

SocialConnect is a project that helps users connect their Instagram Professional accounts, manage Instagram content, publish posts, and test messaging automation features through the Instagram API.

This Privacy Policy explains how SocialConnect collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, SocialConnect may process the following information:

  • Instagram account information: Instagram user ID and username.
  • Authentication information: Access tokens received through Instagram authorization.
  • Content information: Image URLs, captions, post IDs, and publishing status.
  • Messaging information: Sender IDs and message text received through Instagram webhooks when messaging features are enabled.
  • Technical information: Webhook events, error logs, and information needed to operate and troubleshoot the application.

We only intend to process information needed to provide and test the application's features.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Connect and identify an authorized Instagram account.
  • Publish Instagram content at the user's request.
  • Maintain a history of publishing activity.
  • Receive Instagram messaging notifications and send automated replies when the messaging feature is enabled.
  • Troubleshoot errors, improve reliability, and protect the application.

SocialConnect does not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

4. Instagram Permissions

SocialConnect may request Instagram permissions such as instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish, and instagram_business_manage_messages.

These permissions are used only for the corresponding features authorized by the user. Users can revoke the application's access through the applicable Meta or Instagram settings.

Instagram and Meta services are also subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Storage and Security

SocialConnect may store account identifiers, access tokens, post history, and related information in a MongoDB Atlas database used by the application.

Reasonable security measures should be used to protect stored information, including keeping secrets and access tokens out of public source repositories.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing

SocialConnect does not intend to sell or rent personal information. Information may be transmitted to Meta or Instagram when necessary to authenticate accounts, publish content, or provide messaging functionality.

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law or necessary to protect the application's security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the application's functionality, testing, or legitimate operational requirements.

Users who want their information deleted can contact the developer using the contact information below and request deletion of their associated account data, stored access tokens, and publishing history.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where it cannot immediately be removed from backups or external services.

8. Children's Privacy

SocialConnect is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Third-Party Services

The application integrates with Meta and Instagram services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies and terms.

SocialConnect is an independent project and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Meta unless explicitly stated otherwise.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or data practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
